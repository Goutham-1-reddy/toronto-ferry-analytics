# Executive Summary

## Toronto Island Ferry Ticket Sales & Redemption Analytics

### Scope
The dataset contains **261,538 records** from **2015-05-01 to 2025-12-21**.

### Key KPIs
| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Tickets Sold | 12,972,051 |
| Total Tickets Redeemed | 12,785,293 |
| Net Sales minus Redemptions | 186,758 |
| Redemption-to-Sales Ratio | 98.56% |
| Highest individual Sales Count | 7,229 |
| Highest individual Redemption Count | 7,216 |
| Highest-average sales hour | 12:00 |

### Operational use
The dashboard enables stakeholders to filter the historical dataset and inspect demand by time, season, and day type. These insights can support staffing, queue management, capacity planning, and future scheduling analysis.

### Important caveat
The highest individual observation should not automatically be interpreted as the normal peak. Recurring hourly and seasonal patterns are more appropriate for operational planning.
